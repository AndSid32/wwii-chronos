Construction Begins on the First Y-12 Production Building at Oak Ridge

1943-02-18 · ARCHIVE EVENT

Start of a major electromagnetic uranium-isotope separation complex.

Industry, infrastructure and technology changed rapidly between 1938 and 1946, reshaping production, mobility and the material foundations of war and post-war recovery.

On 18 February 1943, in United States, the milestone “Construction Begins on the First Y-12 Production Building at Oak Ridge” occurred. WWII CHRONOS records it as a specific industrial or technological event rather than as a vague multi-year trend.

Verified figure or fact: Start of a major electromagnetic uranium-isotope separation complex. Physical measurements in WWII CHRONOS are expressed in the metric system.

This nuclear industry milestone helps explain the material systems behind the era: factories, energy, transport networks, extraction, communications, new materials and production methods.

The industrial achievement formed part of the atomic-weapons programme. Its engineering scale is historically important, but so are the programme’s military purpose and consequences.

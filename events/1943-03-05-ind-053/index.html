First Flight of the Gloster Meteor

1943-03-05 · ARCHIVE EVENT

The aircraft that would become Britain's first production jet aeroplane.

Industry, infrastructure and technology changed rapidly between 1938 and 1946, reshaping production, mobility and the material foundations of war and post-war recovery.

On 5 March 1943, in United Kingdom, the milestone “First Flight of the Gloster Meteor” occurred. WWII CHRONOS records it as a specific industrial or technological event rather than as a vague multi-year trend.

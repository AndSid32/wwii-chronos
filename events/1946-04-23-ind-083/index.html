Piaggio Registers the Vespa Industrial Model

1946-04-23 · ARCHIVE EVENT

2,484 scooters were produced by the end of 1946.

Industry, infrastructure and technology changed rapidly between 1938 and 1946, reshaping production, mobility and the material foundations of war and post-war recovery.

On 23 April 1946, in Italy, the milestone “Piaggio Registers the Vespa Industrial Model” occurred. WWII CHRONOS records it as a specific industrial or technological event rather than as a vague multi-year trend.

Verified figure or fact: 2,484 scooters were produced by the end of 1946. Physical measurements in WWII CHRONOS are expressed in the metric system.

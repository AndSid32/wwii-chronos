State Defense Committee Launches Large-Scale Evacuation of Soviet Industry Eastward

1941-07-11 · ARCHIVE EVENT

According to consolidated historical data, 1,523 large enterprises were evacuated between July and November.

Industry, infrastructure and technology changed rapidly between 1938 and 1946, reshaping production, mobility and the material foundations of war and post-war recovery.

Hanford Sends Its First Shipment of Plutonium to Los Alamos

1945-02-05 · ARCHIVE EVENT

The new industrial cycle from reactor production to chemical separation is now functioning in practice.

Industry, infrastructure and technology changed rapidly between 1938 and 1946, reshaping production, mobility and the material foundations of war and post-war recovery.

On 5 February 1945, in United States, the milestone “Hanford Sends Its First Shipment of Plutonium to Los Alamos” occurred. WWII CHRONOS records it as a specific industrial or technological event rather than as a vague multi-year trend.

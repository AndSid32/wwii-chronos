Mexico Nationalises the Oil Industry

1938-03-18 · ARCHIVE EVENT

One of the most significant nationalisations of natural resources in the twentieth century.

Industry, infrastructure and technology changed rapidly between 1938 and 1946, reshaping production, mobility and the material foundations of war and post-war recovery.

On 18 March 1938, in Mexico, the milestone “Mexico Nationalises the Oil Industry” occurred. WWII CHRONOS records it as a specific industrial or technological event rather than as a vague multi-year trend.

Verified figure or fact: One of the most significant nationalisations of natural resources in the twentieth century. Physical measurements in WWII CHRONOS are expressed in the metric system.

This oil / economy milestone helps explain the material systems behind the era: factories, energy, transport networks, extraction, communications, new materials and production methods.

Where an industrial achievement was tied to war, coercion or forced labour, the technical accomplishment must be read together with that human and political context rather than celebrated in isolation.

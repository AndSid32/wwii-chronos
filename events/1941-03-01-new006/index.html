Bulgaria Joins the Tripartite Pact

1941-03-01 · ARCHIVE EVENT

In early 1941, diplomatic and military pressure by the Axis powers in the Balkans was increasing. The region was strategically important for future operations against Greece and Yugoslavia.

In early 1941, diplomatic and military pressure by the Axis powers in the Balkans was increasing. The region was strategically important for future operations against Greece and Yugoslavia.

On 1 March 1941, Bulgaria joined the Tripartite Pact, formally aligning itself with the Axis powers.

The decision facilitated the movement of German troops into south-eastern Europe. Bulgaria's participation in particular military operations nevertheless had its own limits.

The decision altered the balance of forces in the Balkans immediately before the German attacks on Yugoslavia and Greece.

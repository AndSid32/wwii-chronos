Polymer Corporation Begins Industrial Production of Synthetic Rubber

1943-09-29 · ARCHIVE EVENT

Later production capacity exceeded about 36,000 t per year.

Industry, infrastructure and technology changed rapidly between 1938 and 1946, reshaping production, mobility and the material foundations of war and post-war recovery.

On 29 September 1943, in Canada, the milestone “Polymer Corporation Begins Industrial Production of Synthetic Rubber” occurred. WWII CHRONOS records it as a specific industrial or technological event rather than as a vague multi-year trend.

Verified figure or fact: Later production capacity exceeded about 36,000 t per year. Physical measurements in WWII CHRONOS are expressed in the metric system.

This chemical industry milestone helps explain the material systems behind the era: factories, energy, transport networks, extraction, communications, new materials and production methods.

Where an industrial achievement was tied to war, coercion or forced labour, the technical accomplishment must be read together with that human and political context rather than celebrated in isolation.

Grand Coulee Sets a Monthly Concrete-Pouring Record

September 1939 · United States

Industry, infrastructure and technology changed rapidly between 1938 and 1946, reshaping production, mobility and the material foundations of war and post-war recovery.

The principal source supports the period September 1939 for “Grand Coulee Sets a Monthly Concrete-Pouring Record”, but it does not justify assigning a single calendar day. WWII CHRONOS therefore preserves the event with a partial date rather than inventing one.

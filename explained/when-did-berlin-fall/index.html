When Did Berlin Fall in 1945?

The short answer: Berlin’s German garrison surrendered to Soviet forces on May 2, 1945, during the final days of the war in Europe.

What happened?

The Soviet offensive toward Berlin began on April 16, 1945. As Soviet troops fought into the city, Adolf Hitler died by suicide on April 30. Berlin surrendered on May 2, six days before Germany’s surrender took effect across Europe.

Who captured Berlin?

The Soviet Red Army captured Berlin. Polish troops fought alongside Soviet forces in the wider campaign. Berlin’s capture was a decisive symbol of the collapse of Nazi Germany, but the formal German surrender followed separately.

Key dates

April 16, 1945Soviet Berlin offensive begins
April 30, 1945Adolf Hitler dies
May 2, 1945Berlin garrison surrenders
May 8, 1945German surrender takes effect

The date May 2 refers to the surrender of Berlin’s garrison, not the surrender of all German forces.

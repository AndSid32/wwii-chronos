When Did World War II End?

The short answer: World War II formally ended on September 2, 1945, with Japan’s signing of the surrender documents. The war in Europe had ended in May 1945.

What happened?

Germany’s surrender took effect on May 8, 1945, commonly known as VE Day. Because of the time difference, the date was already May 9 in Moscow. Japan announced acceptance of Allied surrender terms on August 15; the formal signing followed on September 2.

Why are there different end dates?

Different commemorations refer to different milestones. May 8 marks the end of the war in Europe, August 15 is associated with the announcement of Japan’s surrender, and September 2 marks Japan’s formal signing aboard USS Missouri.

Key dates

May 8, 1945German surrender takes effect
August 15, 1945Japan announces acceptance of surrender terms
September 2, 1945Japanese surrender formally signed

VE Day commemorates the end of the war in Europe, not the end of fighting everywhere.

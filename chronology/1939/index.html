Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1939-01-01 — Hewlett-Packard Founded — The beginning of one of the key manufacturers of electronic measurement and computing equipment.
  2. 1939-03-15 — Declaration of Independence of Carpatho-Ukraine — The Soim of Carpatho-Ukraine proclaimed independence in Khust.
  3. 1939-03-15 — Occupation of the Czech Lands — Germany occupies Bohemia and Moravia after the breakup of Czechoslovakia.
  4. 1939-03-31 — British and French Guarantees to Poland — After the expansion of German power, the British and French governments declared support for Polish independence. European diplomacy increasingly recognised the danger of another war.
  5. 1939-04-07 — Italian Invasion of Albania — Fascist Italy sought to strengthen its influence in the Balkans and control the strategic approaches to the Adriatic Sea. Albania was already under heavy Italian political and economic
  6. 1939-04-30 — RCA Demonstrates Electronic Television to the Public at the New York World's Fair — An important step from experimental television toward a mass medium.
  7. 1939-05-11 — Beginning of the Battles of Khalkhin Gol — A series of clashes began on the border between Mongolia and Manchukuo involving Japanese and Soviet-Mongolian forces.
  8. 1939-05-12 — First Funding for the Harvard Mark I — The automatic electromechanical calculator project received its initial funding.
  9. 1939-05-13 — The MS St. Louis Sails from Hamburg — Jewish refugees attempted to find safety across the Atlantic.
  10. 1939-06-28 — First Regular Transatlantic Passenger Flight by Pan American — 22 passengers and 11 crew aboard the Boeing 314 Dixie Clipper.
  11. 1939-08-23 — Molotov–Ribbentrop Pact — Nazi Germany and the Soviet Union sign a non-aggression pact with a secret protocol dividing spheres of influence.
  12. 1939-08-27 — First Flight of the Jet-Powered Heinkel He 178 — The German experimental Heinkel He 178 made its first flight using turbojet propulsion, an important early demonstration of a powerplant principle that would transform aviation.
  13. 1939-09-01 — First Full-Scale Polyethylene Plant Begins Operation — Polyethylene moves from laboratory material to industrial production.
  14. 1939-09-01 — German Invasion of Poland — The beginning of the Second World War in Europe; Germany attacks from the west.
  15. 1939-09-03 — Britain and France Declare War — The two states enter the war against Germany following the invasion of Poland.
  16. 1939-09-14 — First Flight of the Sikorsky VS-300 Helicopter — A key prototype for the modern single-main-rotor helicopter configuration.
  17. 1939-09-17 — Soviet Invasion of Poland — The Red Army enters eastern Poland in accordance with the Soviet-German division of spheres of influence.
  18. 1939-09-28 — Surrender of Warsaw — After Germany's attack on Poland, Warsaw came under siege. The city's defenders and civilian population endured heavy artillery fire and bombing.
  19. 1939-11-30 — Winter War — The Soviet Union attacks Finland; fighting continues until March 1940.
  20. 1939-12-12 — World's First Industrial Nylon Plant Begins Operation — In its first year, it produced enough nylon for about 64 million pairs of stockings.
  21. 1939-12-13 — Battle of the River Plate — British cruisers engaged the German commerce raider Admiral Graf Spee off the coast of South America.

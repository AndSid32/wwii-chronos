Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1939-01-01 — Hewlett-Packard Founded — The beginning of one of the key manufacturers of electronic measurement and computing equipment.
  2. 1939-04-30 — RCA Demonstrates Electronic Television to the Public at the New York World's Fair — An important step from experimental television toward a mass medium.
  3. 1939-05-12 — First Funding for the Harvard Mark I — The automatic electromechanical calculator project received its initial funding.
  4. 1939-06-28 — First Regular Transatlantic Passenger Flight by Pan American — 22 passengers and 11 crew aboard the Boeing 314 Dixie Clipper.
  5. 1939-08-27 — First Flight of the Jet-Powered Heinkel He 178 — The German experimental Heinkel He 178 made its first flight using turbojet propulsion, an important early demonstration of a powerplant principle that would transform aviation.
  6. 1939-09-01 — First Full-Scale Polyethylene Plant Begins Operation — Polyethylene moves from laboratory material to industrial production.
  7. 1939-09-14 — First Flight of the Sikorsky VS-300 Helicopter — A key prototype for the modern single-main-rotor helicopter configuration.
  8. 1939-12-12 — World's First Industrial Nylon Plant Begins Operation — In its first year, it produced enough nylon for about 64 million pairs of stockings.

Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1944-01-22 — Anzio Landings — American and British forces land behind German defensive positions in Italy.
  2. 1944-01-27 — Siege of Leningrad Fully Lifted — Soviet forces push German troops away from Leningrad and bring the siege of the city to an end.
  3. 1944-02-05 — Colossus Mk I Begins Operation at Bletchley Park — One of the first programmable electronic digital computers.
  4. 1944-03-02 — Little Big Inch Products Pipeline Completed — Together with Big Inch it formed a strategic fuel-delivery system to the eastern United States.
  5. 1944-03-19 — Germany Occupies Hungary — By early 1944, Germany's leadership feared that Hungary might try to leave the Axis alliance.
  6. 1944-04-08 — Crimean Offensive — Soviet forces begin driving German and Romanian troops out of Crimea.
  7. 1944-04-10 — Nazi Forces Driven from Odesa — Soviet forces retake the city during the Odesa Offensive.
  8. 1944-04-14 — Hydro-Québec Created — A key step in centralisation and the later expansion of Quebec's hydroelectric system.
  9. 1944-06-04 — Liberation of Rome — Allied forces enter the Italian capital.
  10. 1944-06-06 — Normandy Landings — D-Day — The Allies open a major front in Western Europe.
  11. 1944-06-19 — Battle of the Philippine Sea — Japanese carrier aviation suffers a major defeat.
  12. 1944-06-22 — Operation Bagration — A massive Soviet offensive begins in Belarus.
  13. 1944-07-01 — Bretton Woods Conference Opens — Delegates work on a new international financial system.
  14. 1944-07-20 — Failed Plot to Assassinate Hitler — A conspiracy by German military and civilian opponents of the regime fails.
  15. 1944-07-25 — Allied Breakout from Normandy — Operation Cobra — After the 6 June 1944 landings, Allied troops fought for weeks in Normandy and struggled to advance rapidly into France.
  16. 1944-07-25 — First Confirmed Air Combat by the Jet-Powered Me 262 — Jet aviation moves from testing into combat use.
  17. 1944-08-01 — Warsaw Uprising — The Polish Home Army begins an uprising against German occupation in Warsaw.
  18. 1944-08-07 — Harvard Mark I Presented to Harvard University — IBM formally presents one of the early automatic digital calculators to the university.
  19. 1944-08-15 — Allied Landings in Southern France — Allied troops land on France's Mediterranean coast.
  20. 1944-08-21 — Dumbarton Oaks Conference Begins — Representatives of major powers develop plans for the future United Nations.
  21. 1944-08-25 — Liberation of Paris — French and Allied forces restore control over Paris.
  22. 1944-09-01 — Volvo Unveils the PV444 — The model laid the foundation for Volvo's post-war mass car production.
  23. 1944-09-09 — Willow Run Produces Its 6,000th B-24 — At peak output: approximately one B-24 every 63 minutes.
  24. 1944-09-17 — Operation Market Garden — The Allies attempt to seize bridges in the Netherlands with airborne troops and a ground advance.
  25. 1944-09-22 — First PLUTO Pipeline to Cherbourg Begins Delivering Fuel — Initial throughput: approximately 255,000 litres per day.
  26. 1944-09-26 — Hanford B Reactor Reaches Criticality — The first large-scale reactor for industrial plutonium production.
  27. 1944-10-02 — Warsaw Uprising Ends — After two months of fighting, Polish insurgents end organised resistance.
  28. 1944-10-06 — Fighting at the Dukla Pass — Soviet and Czechoslovak troops enter Slovakia.
  29. 1944-10-07 — Sonderkommando Uprising at Auschwitz — Prisoners of the Sonderkommando stage an uprising at Auschwitz-Birkenau.
  30. 1944-10-20 — Landings on Leyte — American forces begin the return to the Philippines with landings on Leyte.
  31. 1944-10-23 — Battle of Leyte Gulf — One of the largest naval battles in history.
  32. 1944-12-16 — Battle of the Bulge — Germany launches its last major offensive on the Western Front.

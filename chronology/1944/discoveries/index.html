Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1944-02-05 — Colossus Mk I Begins Operation at Bletchley Park — One of the first programmable electronic digital computers.
  2. 1944-03-02 — Little Big Inch Products Pipeline Completed — Together with Big Inch it formed a strategic fuel-delivery system to the eastern United States.
  3. 1944-04-14 — Hydro-Québec Created — A key step in centralisation and the later expansion of Quebec's hydroelectric system.
  4. 1944-07-25 — First Confirmed Air Combat by the Jet-Powered Me 262 — Jet aviation moves from testing into combat use.
  5. 1944-08-07 — Harvard Mark I Presented to Harvard University — IBM formally presents one of the early automatic digital calculators to the university.
  6. 1944-09-01 — Volvo Unveils the PV444 — The model laid the foundation for Volvo's post-war mass car production.
  7. 1944-09-09 — Willow Run Produces Its 6,000th B-24 — At peak output: approximately one B-24 every 63 minutes.
  8. 1944-09-22 — First PLUTO Pipeline to Cherbourg Begins Delivering Fuel — Initial throughput: approximately 255,000 litres per day.
  9. 1944-09-26 — Hanford B Reactor Reaches Criticality — The first large-scale reactor for industrial plutonium production.

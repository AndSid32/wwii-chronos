Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1941-05-12 — Programmable Z3 Computer Operates — Konrad Zuse demonstrated the Z3, a programmable electromechanical computing machine. It used relays and read its program from punched film.
  2. 1941-05-15 — First Flight of Britain's Gloster E.28/39 Jet Aircraft — The first flight lasted 17 minutes; a key milestone for Frank Whittle's jet engine.
  3. 1941-05-20 — Battle of Crete — German airborne forces begin an operation to seize the island.
  4. 1941-05-24 — Battle of the Denmark Strait — The German battleship Bismarck sinks the British battlecruiser HMS Hood.

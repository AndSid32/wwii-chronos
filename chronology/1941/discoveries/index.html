Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1941-01-14 — Construction Begins on Kaiser's First Richmond Shipyard — The start of one of the major US centres of assembly-line shipbuilding.
  2. 1941-04-09 — Companhia Siderúrgica Nacional (CSN) Established — The foundation for large-scale integrated steel production in Brazil.
  3. 1941-04-24 — Detroit Tank Arsenal Produces Its First Production Tank — Automotive mass-production methods are transferred to heavy armoured vehicles.
  4. 1941-05-12 — Programmable Z3 Computer Operates — Konrad Zuse demonstrated the Z3, a programmable electromechanical computing machine. It used relays and read its program from punched film.
  5. 1941-05-15 — First Flight of Britain's Gloster E.28/39 Jet Aircraft — The first flight lasted 17 minutes; a key milestone for Frank Whittle's jet engine.
  6. 1941-07-11 — State Defense Committee Launches Large-Scale Evacuation of Soviet Industry Eastward — According to consolidated historical data, 1,523 large enterprises were evacuated between July and November.
  7. 1941-07-15 — Willys-Overland Receives Contract for Mass Production of the Military Jeep — The Jeep design is standardised for mass production.
  8. 1941-09-27 — First Liberty Ship — SS Patrick Henry — Launched — Typical series length: about 134.6 m; deadweight about 10,800 t; more than 2,700 ships built during the war.
  9. 1941-10-04 — First Grand Coulee Generator Begins Operation — Rated output of the first generator: 108 MW.

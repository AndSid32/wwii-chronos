Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1941-01-14 — Construction Begins on Kaiser's First Richmond Shipyard — The start of one of the major US centres of assembly-line shipbuilding.
  2. 1941-02-12 — Rommel Arrives in North Africa — German forces begin deploying to support Italy in North Africa.
  3. 1941-03-01 — Bulgaria Joins the Tripartite Pact — In early 1941, diplomatic and military pressure by the Axis powers in the Balkans was increasing. The region was strategically important for future operations against Greece and Yugosla
  4. 1941-03-11 — Lend-Lease Act — The United States adopts legislation authorising the supply of military materials to Allied countries.
  5. 1941-04-06 — Invasion of Yugoslavia and Greece — Germany and its allies begin the Balkan campaign.
  6. 1941-04-09 — Companhia Siderúrgica Nacional (CSN) Established — The foundation for large-scale integrated steel production in Brazil.
  7. 1941-04-10 — Proclamation of the Independent State of Croatia — After the Axis invasion of Yugoslavia began, the country's political and military system collapsed rapidly.
  8. 1941-04-24 — Detroit Tank Arsenal Produces Its First Production Tank — Automotive mass-production methods are transferred to heavy armoured vehicles.
  9. 1941-05-12 — Programmable Z3 Computer Operates — Konrad Zuse demonstrated the Z3, a programmable electromechanical computing machine. It used relays and read its program from punched film.
  10. 1941-05-15 — First Flight of Britain's Gloster E.28/39 Jet Aircraft — The first flight lasted 17 minutes; a key milestone for Frank Whittle's jet engine.
  11. 1941-05-20 — Battle of Crete — German airborne forces begin an operation to seize the island.
  12. 1941-05-24 — Battle of the Denmark Strait — The German battleship Bismarck sinks the British battlecruiser HMS Hood.
  13. 1941-06-22 — Operation Barbarossa — Nazi Germany and its allies attack the Soviet Union, opening the largest land front of the war.
  14. 1941-07-06 — Mass Murder of Jews at Kaunas Seventh Fort — One of the early large-scale mass shootings following the invasion of the Soviet Union.
  15. 1941-07-11 — State Defense Committee Launches Large-Scale Evacuation of Soviet Industry Eastward — According to consolidated historical data, 1,523 large enterprises were evacuated between July and November.
  16. 1941-07-12 — Anglo-Soviet Agreement on Joint Action — Britain and the Soviet Union agree to cooperate against Nazi Germany.
  17. 1941-07-15 — Willys-Overland Receives Contract for Mass Production of the Military Jeep — The Jeep design is standardised for mass production.
  18. 1941-08-03 — Bishop von Galen's Sermon against the Murder of Patients — The Catholic bishop publicly condemned the Nazi 'euthanasia' programme.
  19. 1941-08-14 — Atlantic Charter — Roosevelt and Churchill published principles for the post-war international order.
  20. 1941-09-08 — Beginning of the Siege of Leningrad — Land routes to Leningrad are cut; the siege will cause mass civilian death.
  21. 1941-09-19 — German Forces Capture Kyiv — The capital of the Ukrainian SSR falls under German occupation.
  22. 1941-09-27 — First Liberty Ship — SS Patrick Henry — Launched — Typical series length: about 134.6 m; deadweight about 10,800 t; more than 2,700 ships built during the war.
  23. 1941-09-29 — Babyn Yar in Kyiv — On 29–30 September, Nazi occupiers shoot tens of thousands of Kyiv's Jews.
  24. 1941-10-02 — Operation Typhoon — The German offensive toward Moscow enters its decisive phase.
  25. 1941-10-04 — First Grand Coulee Generator Begins Operation — Rated output of the first generator: 108 MW.
  26. 1941-11-18 — Operation Crusader — The British Eighth Army goes on the offensive in North Africa.
  27. 1941-12-05 — Soviet Counteroffensive at Moscow — Soviet forces begin a large-scale counteroffensive around Moscow.
  28. 1941-12-07 — Attack on Pearl Harbor — Japan strikes the US naval base in Hawaii.
  29. 1941-12-08 — Mass Killing Begins at Chełmno — The killing centre began using gas vans.
  30. 1941-12-08 — United States Enters the War against Japan — The US Congress declares war on Japan as the Japanese offensive continues across the Pacific.
  31. 1941-12-11 — Germany Declares War on the United States — The European and Pacific wars become even more closely connected.

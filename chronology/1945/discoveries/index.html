Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1945-01-16 — Renault Nationalised — The state restructures one of France's major automobile manufacturers after the occupation.
  2. 1945-01-20 — First K-25 Production Cell Becomes Operational — The full arrangement had 54 main production units; all were operating by 15 August 1945.
  3. 1945-02-05 — Hanford Sends Its First Shipment of Plutonium to Los Alamos — The new industrial cycle from reactor production to chemical separation is now functioning in practice.
  4. 1945-06-22 — First Flight of the Vickers Viking Passenger Aircraft — One of Britain's first new passenger aircraft of the post-war era.
  5. 1945-06-28 — Willow Run Produces Its Final B-24 — The plant built 8,685 B-24 bombers.
  6. 1945-09-05 — ZEEP Reactor Reaches Criticality for the First Time — The first nuclear reactor outside the United States to achieve a self-sustaining chain reaction.
  7. 1945-09-20 — Rolls-Royce Trent Makes Its First Flight as an Early Turboprop Engine — A key early milestone in turboprop transport aviation.
  8. 1945-09-25 — First Flight of the de Havilland Dove — A symbol of aircraft factories shifting toward post-war civil production.
  9. 1945-10-24 — UN Charter Enters into Force — The Charter of the United Nations, signed in San Francisco on 26 June 1945, enters into force on 24 October, marking the formal beginning of the United Nations.
  10. 1945-11-16 — UNESCO Constitution Adopted — States agree on the founding principles of an international organisation for cooperation in education, science and culture.
  11. 1945-12-27 — Series Production of Volkswagen Resumes at Wolfsburg — 55 cars were produced by the end of 1945.

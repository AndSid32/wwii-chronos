Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1945-02-19 — Battle of Iwo Jima — American forces land on the Japanese island.
  2. 1945-04-01 — Battle of Okinawa — American landings on Okinawa begin a prolonged battle.
  3. 1945-06-22 — Battle of Okinawa Ends — Organised Japanese resistance on Okinawa comes to an end.
  4. 1945-08-08 — Soviet Union Enters the War against Japan — The Soviet Union declares war on Japan; the offensive in Manchuria begins on 9 August.
  5. 1945-08-14 — Japan Agrees to Surrender — In August 1945, Japan faced the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki and the Soviet entry into the war in the Far East.
  6. 1945-08-15 — Japan Announces Surrender — The Emperor of Japan informs the population that the surrender terms have been accepted.
  7. 1945-08-17 — Indonesian Declaration of Independence — Sukarno and Mohammad Hatta proclaim Indonesian independence after Japan's surrender.
  8. 1945-09-02 — Formal Surrender of Japan — The surrender instrument is signed aboard USS Missouri, bringing the Second World War to a formal end.

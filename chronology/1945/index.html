Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1945-01-12 — Vistula–Oder Offensive — Soviet forces begin a broad offensive on the road to Berlin.
  2. 1945-01-16 — Renault Nationalised — The state restructures one of France's major automobile manufacturers after the occupation.
  3. 1945-01-20 — First K-25 Production Cell Becomes Operational — The full arrangement had 54 main production units; all were operating by 15 August 1945.
  4. 1945-01-27 — Liberation of Auschwitz — The Red Army enters the Auschwitz concentration and extermination camp complex.
  5. 1945-02-04 — Yalta Conference — The leaders of the Soviet Union, the United States and Britain discuss the end of the war and post-war Europe.
  6. 1945-02-05 — Hanford Sends Its First Shipment of Plutonium to Los Alamos — The new industrial cycle from reactor production to chemical separation is now functioning in practice.
  7. 1945-02-13 — Bombing of Dresden — British and American aircraft carry out mass air raids on the German city of Dresden.
  8. 1945-02-19 — Battle of Iwo Jima — American forces land on the Japanese island.
  9. 1945-03-07 — Crossing of the Rhine at Remagen — American forces capture a bridge across the Rhine.
  10. 1945-03-09 — Firebombing of Tokyo — During the night of 9–10 March, American bombers carry out a devastating incendiary attack on Tokyo.
  11. 1945-04-01 — Battle of Okinawa — American landings on Okinawa begin a prolonged battle.
  12. 1945-04-12 — Death of Franklin D. Roosevelt — The US president dies during the final campaigns of the Second World War.
  13. 1945-04-16 — Berlin Strategic Offensive Operation — Soviet forces begin the final offensive on the capital of Nazi Germany.
  14. 1945-04-25 — Meeting on the Elbe — American and Soviet troops meet near Torgau.
  15. 1945-04-25 — San Francisco Conference Opens — Delegates begin discussing the Charter of the United Nations.
  16. 1945-04-30 — Suicide of Adolf Hitler — Adolf Hitler dies in Berlin as the Nazi regime collapses.
  17. 1945-05-07 — German Surrender Signed at Reims — By spring 1945, German forces were losing control across Europe. The leadership of the Third Reich could no longer continue the war on two fronts.
  18. 1945-05-08 — German Surrender Takes Effect — The act of unconditional surrender takes effect on 8 May in Central European time, corresponding to 9 May in Moscow time.
  19. 1945-06-22 — Battle of Okinawa Ends — Organised Japanese resistance on Okinawa comes to an end.
  20. 1945-06-22 — First Flight of the Vickers Viking Passenger Aircraft — One of Britain's first new passenger aircraft of the post-war era.
  21. 1945-06-26 — UN Charter Signed — Delegates at the San Francisco Conference sign the Charter of the United Nations.
  22. 1945-06-28 — Willow Run Produces Its Final B-24 — The plant built 8,685 B-24 bombers.
  23. 1945-07-16 — Trinity Nuclear Test — The United States conducts the world's first detonation of a nuclear device in New Mexico.
  24. 1945-07-17 — Potsdam Conference — The Allied leaders discuss the post-war settlement.
  25. 1945-08-06 — Atomic Bombing of Hiroshima — The United States drops an atomic bomb on the Japanese city, killing large numbers of civilians.
  26. 1945-08-08 — Soviet Union Enters the War against Japan — The Soviet Union declares war on Japan; the offensive in Manchuria begins on 9 August.
  27. 1945-08-09 — Atomic Bombing of Nagasaki — A second American atomic bomb strikes the Japanese city.
  28. 1945-08-14 — Japan Agrees to Surrender — In August 1945, Japan faced the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki and the Soviet entry into the war in the Far East.
  29. 1945-08-15 — Japan Announces Surrender — The Emperor of Japan informs the population that the surrender terms have been accepted.
  30. 1945-08-17 — Indonesian Declaration of Independence — Sukarno and Mohammad Hatta proclaim Indonesian independence after Japan's surrender.
  31. 1945-09-02 — Formal Surrender of Japan — The surrender instrument is signed aboard USS Missouri, bringing the Second World War to a formal end.
  32. 1945-09-05 — ZEEP Reactor Reaches Criticality for the First Time — The first nuclear reactor outside the United States to achieve a self-sustaining chain reaction.
  33. 1945-09-20 — Rolls-Royce Trent Makes Its First Flight as an Early Turboprop Engine — A key early milestone in turboprop transport aviation.
  34. 1945-09-25 — First Flight of the de Havilland Dove — A symbol of aircraft factories shifting toward post-war civil production.
  35. 1945-10-24 — UN Charter Enters into Force — The Charter of the United Nations, signed in San Francisco on 26 June 1945, enters into force on 24 October, marking the formal beginning of the United Nations.
  36. 1945-10-24 — United Nations Formally Established — The Charter of the United Nations enters into force.
  37. 1945-11-16 — UNESCO Constitution Adopted — States agree on the founding principles of an international organisation for cooperation in education, science and culture.
  38. 1945-11-20 — Nuremberg Trials Begin — The International Military Tribunal opens proceedings against major Nazi leaders.
  39. 1945-12-27 — Series Production of Volkswagen Resumes at Wolfsburg — 55 cars were produced by the end of 1945.

Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1945-02-04 — Yalta Conference — The leaders of the Soviet Union, the United States and Britain discuss the end of the war and post-war Europe.
  2. 1945-04-12 — Death of Franklin D. Roosevelt — The US president dies during the final campaigns of the Second World War.
  3. 1945-04-25 — Meeting on the Elbe — American and Soviet troops meet near Torgau.
  4. 1945-04-25 — San Francisco Conference Opens — Delegates begin discussing the Charter of the United Nations.
  5. 1945-05-07 — German Surrender Signed at Reims — By spring 1945, German forces were losing control across Europe. The leadership of the Third Reich could no longer continue the war on two fronts.
  6. 1945-06-26 — UN Charter Signed — Delegates at the San Francisco Conference sign the Charter of the United Nations.
  7. 1945-07-16 — Trinity Nuclear Test — The United States conducts the world's first detonation of a nuclear device in New Mexico.
  8. 1945-07-17 — Potsdam Conference — The Allied leaders discuss the post-war settlement.
  9. 1945-10-24 — United Nations Formally Established — The Charter of the United Nations enters into force.
  10. 1945-11-20 — Nuremberg Trials Begin — The International Military Tribunal opens proceedings against major Nazi leaders.

Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1938-01-01 — Creation of SNCF — Unification of France's Main Railways — The state received 51% of the new company's capital.
  2. 1938-03-04 — Dammam No. 7 Strikes Oil in Commercial Quantities — The commercial discovery that launched Saudi Arabia's oil era.
  3. 1938-03-18 — Mexico Nationalises the Oil Industry — One of the most significant nationalisations of natural resources in the twentieth century.
  4. 1938-04-01 — Nestlé Launches Nescafé — Industrial instant-coffee technology reaches the mass market.
  5. 1938-04-06 — Discovery of Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) — A new polymer with exceptional chemical resistance and very low friction.
  6. 1938-05-23 — Technical Design of the Uglich and Rybinsk Hydroelectric Complexes Approved — Uglich HPP design capacity: 110 MW with two generating units.
  7. 1938-05-26 — Foundation Stone Laid for Volkswagen's Main Plant — A project for one of the largest mass-production automobile factories of its time.
  8. 1938-07-03 — Mallard Sets the World Steam Locomotive Speed Record — Maximum speed: approximately 203 km/h.
  9. 1938-10-22 — Chester Carlson Produces the First Xerographic Image — The technological basis for future mass dry-copying of documents.
  10. 1938-10-30 — Orson Welles's 'The War of the Worlds' Radio Broadcast — The radio drama demonstrated both the power and the risks of realistic audio journalism.
  11. 1939-01-01 — Hewlett-Packard Founded — The beginning of one of the key manufacturers of electronic measurement and computing equipment.
  12. 1939-04-30 — RCA Demonstrates Electronic Television to the Public at the New York World's Fair — An important step from experimental television toward a mass medium.
  13. 1939-05-12 — First Funding for the Harvard Mark I — The automatic electromechanical calculator project received its initial funding.
  14. 1939-06-28 — First Regular Transatlantic Passenger Flight by Pan American — 22 passengers and 11 crew aboard the Boeing 314 Dixie Clipper.
  15. 1939-08-27 — First Flight of the Jet-Powered Heinkel He 178 — The German experimental Heinkel He 178 made its first flight using turbojet propulsion, an important early demonstration of a powerplant principle that would transform aviation.
  16. 1939-09-01 — First Full-Scale Polyethylene Plant Begins Operation — Polyethylene moves from laboratory material to industrial production.
  17. 1939-09-14 — First Flight of the Sikorsky VS-300 Helicopter — A key prototype for the modern single-main-rotor helicopter configuration.
  18. 1939-12-12 — World's First Industrial Nylon Plant Begins Operation — In its first year, it produced enough nylon for about 64 million pairs of stockings.
  19. 1940-02-21 — High-Power Cavity Magnetron Successfully Tested in Birmingham — The technological foundation for compact microwave radar.
  20. 1940-02-23 — Russell Ohl Observes the p–n Junction in Silicon — A fundamental milestone for the future semiconductor industry.
  21. 1940-05-15 — Nylon Stockings Go on Mass Sale — Synthetic fibres enter the mass consumer market.
  22. 1940-08-27 — First Flight of the Caproni Campini N.1 Experimental Jet Aircraft — One of the earliest full-scale demonstrations of jet propulsion.
  23. 1940-08-29 — Marl Plant Produces Its First Bales of Buna Synthetic Rubber — Initial annual capacity: approximately 18,000 t.
  24. 1940-09-11 — Bell Labs Publicly Demonstrates Remote Computing over Telephone Lines — An early demonstration of remote access to a computing machine.
  25. 1940-09-23 — American Bantam Delivers the First Prototype of the Future Jeep — 49 days elapsed from order to prototype delivery.
  26. 1941-01-14 — Construction Begins on Kaiser's First Richmond Shipyard — The start of one of the major US centres of assembly-line shipbuilding.
  27. 1941-04-09 — Companhia Siderúrgica Nacional (CSN) Established — The foundation for large-scale integrated steel production in Brazil.
  28. 1941-04-24 — Detroit Tank Arsenal Produces Its First Production Tank — Automotive mass-production methods are transferred to heavy armoured vehicles.
  29. 1941-05-12 — Programmable Z3 Computer Operates — Konrad Zuse demonstrated the Z3, a programmable electromechanical computing machine. It used relays and read its program from punched film.
  30. 1941-05-15 — First Flight of Britain's Gloster E.28/39 Jet Aircraft — The first flight lasted 17 minutes; a key milestone for Frank Whittle's jet engine.
  31. 1941-07-11 — State Defense Committee Launches Large-Scale Evacuation of Soviet Industry Eastward — According to consolidated historical data, 1,523 large enterprises were evacuated between July and November.
  32. 1941-07-15 — Willys-Overland Receives Contract for Mass Production of the Military Jeep — The Jeep design is standardised for mass production.
  33. 1941-09-27 — First Liberty Ship — SS Patrick Henry — Launched — Typical series length: about 134.6 m; deadweight about 10,800 t; more than 2,700 ships built during the war.
  34. 1941-10-04 — First Grand Coulee Generator Begins Operation — Rated output of the first generator: 108 MW.
  35. 1942-01-14 — First Flight of the Sikorsky XR-4 — Prototype of the First Production Helicopter — In April, the XR-4 reached about 1,520 m; during a delivery flight it covered about 1,225 km.
  36. 1942-05-25 — First Commercial Fluid Catalytic Cracking Reactor Starts Up — Feed throughput: about 2.23 million litres per day.
  37. 1942-07-18 — Messerschmitt Me 262 Makes Its First Flight on Jet Power Alone — A key milestone for the first production jet fighter.
  38. 1942-08-13 — Manhattan Engineer District Formally Established — Organisational start of the Oak Ridge and Hanford industrial sites.
  39. 1942-10-03 — First Successful Full-Scale Test of the V-2 Rocket — Length about 14 m; mass about 13.2 t; speed over 5,600 km/h.
  40. 1942-11-12 — Liberty Ship SS Robert E. Peary Sets a Construction-Speed Record — From keel laying to launch: 4 days, 15 hours, 29 minutes.
  41. 1942-11-20 — Alaska Highway Officially Opened — Original route: more than 2,400 km.
  42. 1942-12-02 — First Controlled Self-Sustaining Nuclear Chain Reaction — Under Enrico Fermi's leadership, Chicago Pile-1 achieved the first controlled self-sustaining nuclear chain reaction, a major milestone in experimental nuclear physics.
  43. 1943-01-09 — First Flight of the Lockheed Constellation — A future symbol of post-war long-range piston-powered aviation.
  44. 1943-02-18 — Construction Begins on the First Y-12 Production Building at Oak Ridge — Start of a major electromagnetic uranium-isotope separation complex.
  45. 1943-03-05 — First Flight of the Gloster Meteor — The aircraft that would become Britain's first production jet aeroplane.
  46. 1943-03-22 — Major Construction Begins at the Hanford Industrial Complex — Start of the first complex for large-scale plutonium production.
  47. 1943-04-09 — Contract Signed to Build ENIAC — The Moore School of Electrical Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania and the US Army sign a contract to develop the ENIAC electronic computer. One of its intended tasks is artil
  48. 1943-06-29 — First Flight of the First Production B-29 Built at Wichita — A milestone for one of the most complex mass-produced aircraft of the war.
  49. 1943-08-14 — Big Inch Oil Pipeline Completed — The trunk line stretched for more than 2,000 km.
  50. 1943-09-29 — Polymer Corporation Begins Industrial Production of Synthetic Rubber — Later production capacity exceeded about 36,000 t per year.
  51. 1943-10-19 — Discovery of the Antibiotic Streptomycin — Researchers at Rutgers identified a substance that became important in the treatment of tuberculosis.
  52. 1943-11-03 — Willow Run Completes Its 1,000th B-24 Bomber — The 1,000th completed B-24; a precursor to the peak assembly-line rate reached in 1944.
  53. 1943-11-04 — X-10 Graphite Reactor Begins Operation at Oak Ridge — The first reactor to produce plutonium continuously on a pilot-industrial scale.
  54. 1944-02-05 — Colossus Mk I Begins Operation at Bletchley Park — One of the first programmable electronic digital computers.
  55. 1944-03-02 — Little Big Inch Products Pipeline Completed — Together with Big Inch it formed a strategic fuel-delivery system to the eastern United States.
  56. 1944-04-14 — Hydro-Québec Created — A key step in centralisation and the later expansion of Quebec's hydroelectric system.
  57. 1944-07-25 — First Confirmed Air Combat by the Jet-Powered Me 262 — Jet aviation moves from testing into combat use.
  58. 1944-08-07 — Harvard Mark I Presented to Harvard University — IBM formally presents one of the early automatic digital calculators to the university.
  59. 1944-09-01 — Volvo Unveils the PV444 — The model laid the foundation for Volvo's post-war mass car production.
  60. 1944-09-09 — Willow Run Produces Its 6,000th B-24 — At peak output: approximately one B-24 every 63 minutes.
  61. 1944-09-22 — First PLUTO Pipeline to Cherbourg Begins Delivering Fuel — Initial throughput: approximately 255,000 litres per day.
  62. 1944-09-26 — Hanford B Reactor Reaches Criticality — The first large-scale reactor for industrial plutonium production.
  63. 1945-01-16 — Renault Nationalised — The state restructures one of France's major automobile manufacturers after the occupation.
  64. 1945-01-20 — First K-25 Production Cell Becomes Operational — The full arrangement had 54 main production units; all were operating by 15 August 1945.
  65. 1945-02-05 — Hanford Sends Its First Shipment of Plutonium to Los Alamos — The new industrial cycle from reactor production to chemical separation is now functioning in practice.
  66. 1945-06-22 — First Flight of the Vickers Viking Passenger Aircraft — One of Britain's first new passenger aircraft of the post-war era.
  67. 1945-06-28 — Willow Run Produces Its Final B-24 — The plant built 8,685 B-24 bombers.
  68. 1945-09-05 — ZEEP Reactor Reaches Criticality for the First Time — The first nuclear reactor outside the United States to achieve a self-sustaining chain reaction.
  69. 1945-09-20 — Rolls-Royce Trent Makes Its First Flight as an Early Turboprop Engine — A key early milestone in turboprop transport aviation.
  70. 1945-09-25 — First Flight of the de Havilland Dove — A symbol of aircraft factories shifting toward post-war civil production.
  71. 1945-10-24 — UN Charter Enters into Force — The Charter of the United Nations, signed in San Francisco on 26 June 1945, enters into force on 24 October, marking the formal beginning of the United Nations.
  72. 1945-11-16 — UNESCO Constitution Adopted — States agree on the founding principles of an international organisation for cooperation in education, science and culture.
  73. 1945-12-27 — Series Production of Volkswagen Resumes at Wolfsburg — 55 cars were produced by the end of 1945.
  74. 1946-02-14 — ENIAC Publicly Unveiled — The United States unveils ENIAC, a large-scale electronic digital computer whose calculation speed greatly exceeded that of most contemporary electromechanical systems.
  75. 1946-03-08 — Bell 47 Receives the First Civil Helicopter Certification — It opened the civilian production market for helicopters.
  76. 1946-04-08 — Électricité de France (EDF) Created — More than 1,650 electricity and gas companies were consolidated.
  77. 1946-04-23 — Piaggio Registers the Vespa Industrial Model — 2,484 scooters were produced by the end of 1946.
  78. 1946-05-07 — Tokyo Tsushin Kogyo — the Future Sony — Founded — The beginning of one of the emblematic companies of Japan's high-technology industry.
  79. 1946-06-17 — First Commercial Mobile Telephone Service Launched — Practical mobile telephony decades before cellular networks.
  80. 1946-08-01 — Atomic Energy Act of 1946 Signed — The US atomic complex shifts from wartime to civilian federal administration.
  81. 1946-08-02 — First Official Shipment of Reactor-Produced Radioisotopes Sent for Civilian Use — Reactor technology begins moving into medicine, biology and industrial research.
  82. 1946-09-01 — Vickers Viking Enters Regular Service with British European Airways — A new production passenger aircraft for rebuilding Europe's air network.
  83. 1946-09-20 — First Cannes Film Festival Opens — After the wartime interruption, Cannes hosts an international programme of screenings and cultural events.
  84. 1946-10-03 — Renault 4CV Publicly Unveiled at the Reopened Paris Motor Show — One of the symbols of post-war mass motorisation in Europe.
  85. 1946-11-04 — UNESCO Constitution Enters into Force — After the required acceptances, UNESCO's founding document acquires legal force.

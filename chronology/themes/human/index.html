Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1938-05-29 — First Anti-Jewish Law in Hungary — The law restricted Jewish participation in economic life.
  2. 1938-07-06 — Opening of the Évian Conference on Jewish Refugees — Representatives of states discussed the refugee crisis created by persecution in Nazi-controlled territories.
  3. 1938-11-09 — Kristallnacht — Mass anti-Jewish pogroms across Germany and annexed Austria on 9–10 November.
  4. 1938-12-02 — First Kindertransport Arrives in Britain — Groups of child refugees from Nazi-controlled territories began arriving in Britain.
  5. 1939-05-13 — The MS St. Louis Sails from Hamburg — Jewish refugees attempted to find safety across the Atlantic.
  6. 1940-04-30 — Sealing of the Łódź Ghetto — The German administration isolated the city's Jewish population.
  7. 1940-11-15 — Warsaw Ghetto Sealed — The German occupation authorities isolate Jews inside the Warsaw ghetto.
  8. 1941-07-06 — Mass Murder of Jews at Kaunas Seventh Fort — One of the early large-scale mass shootings following the invasion of the Soviet Union.
  9. 1941-08-03 — Bishop von Galen's Sermon against the Murder of Patients — The Catholic bishop publicly condemned the Nazi 'euthanasia' programme.
  10. 1941-09-29 — Babyn Yar in Kyiv — On 29–30 September, Nazi occupiers shoot tens of thousands of Kyiv's Jews.
  11. 1941-12-08 — Mass Killing Begins at Chełmno — The killing centre began using gas vans.
  12. 1942-01-16 — Deportations from the Łódź Ghetto to Chełmno Begin — The Nazis begin systematic deportations of Jews to be murdered.
  13. 1942-01-20 — Wannsee Conference — Nazi officials coordinate the implementation of the policy to systematically murder Europe's Jews.
  14. 1942-03-27 — Deportations of Jews from France to Auschwitz Begin — Deportations from occupied France were directed mainly to Auschwitz.
  15. 1942-07-15 — Systematic Deportations of Jews from the Netherlands Begin — Transports departed for the killing centres.
  16. 1942-07-22 — Grossaktion in the Warsaw Ghetto Begins — Mass deportations to Treblinka begin.
  17. 1943-04-19 — Warsaw Ghetto Uprising — Jewish underground fighters begin armed resistance to the Nazi liquidation of the ghetto.
  18. 1943-10-01 — Rescue of the Danish Jews — The Danish resistance and ordinary citizens begin a mass effort to ferry Jews to Sweden.
  19. 1944-10-07 — Sonderkommando Uprising at Auschwitz — Prisoners of the Sonderkommando stage an uprising at Auschwitz-Birkenau.
  20. 1945-01-27 — Liberation of Auschwitz — The Red Army enters the Auschwitz concentration and extermination camp complex.
  21. 1946-07-04 — Kielce Pogrom — Anti-Jewish violence in post-war Poland kills dozens of people.

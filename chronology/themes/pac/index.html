Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1938-07-29 — Battles at Lake Khasan — Fighting broke out between the Red Army and forces of the Japanese Empire along the Soviet-Korean border.
  2. 1939-05-11 — Beginning of the Battles of Khalkhin Gol — A series of clashes began on the border between Mongolia and Manchukuo involving Japanese and Soviet-Mongolian forces.
  3. 1941-12-07 — Attack on Pearl Harbor — Japan strikes the US naval base in Hawaii.
  4. 1941-12-08 — United States Enters the War against Japan — The US Congress declares war on Japan as the Japanese offensive continues across the Pacific.
  5. 1942-01-02 — Japanese Forces Capture Manila — Japanese forces enter the Philippine capital; American and Filipino troops withdraw to Bataan.
  6. 1942-01-23 — Japanese Landing at Rabaul — Japanese forces begin the capture of Rabaul on New Britain.
  7. 1942-01-31 — Allied Forces Leave the Malay Peninsula — British and Allied units withdraw to Singapore after the Japanese advance through Malaya.
  8. 1942-02-15 — Fall of Singapore — The British garrison at Singapore surrenders to Japanese forces.
  9. 1942-03-01 — Second Battle of the Java Sea — Japanese forces destroy Allied ships attempting to escape the Java area.
  10. 1942-04-09 — Surrender on Bataan — American and Filipino forces surrender to Japan; the Bataan Death March follows.
  11. 1942-05-04 — Battle of the Coral Sea — US and Japanese carrier forces fight a battle in which the opposing surface fleets never directly exchange gunfire.
  12. 1942-06-04 — Battle of Midway — The US Navy wins a major victory over Japanese carrier forces.
  13. 1942-08-07 — Landings on Guadalcanal — The United States begins an offensive operation in the Solomon Islands.
  14. 1943-02-09 — Battle of Guadalcanal Ends — The United States secures control of the island after Japanese forces withdraw.
  15. 1944-06-19 — Battle of the Philippine Sea — Japanese carrier aviation suffers a major defeat.
  16. 1944-10-20 — Landings on Leyte — American forces begin the return to the Philippines with landings on Leyte.
  17. 1944-10-23 — Battle of Leyte Gulf — One of the largest naval battles in history.
  18. 1945-02-19 — Battle of Iwo Jima — American forces land on the Japanese island.
  19. 1945-04-01 — Battle of Okinawa — American landings on Okinawa begin a prolonged battle.
  20. 1945-06-22 — Battle of Okinawa Ends — Organised Japanese resistance on Okinawa comes to an end.
  21. 1945-08-08 — Soviet Union Enters the War against Japan — The Soviet Union declares war on Japan; the offensive in Manchuria begins on 9 August.
  22. 1945-08-14 — Japan Agrees to Surrender — In August 1945, Japan faced the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki and the Soviet entry into the war in the Far East.
  23. 1945-08-15 — Japan Announces Surrender — The Emperor of Japan informs the population that the surrender terms have been accepted.
  24. 1945-08-17 — Indonesian Declaration of Independence — Sukarno and Mohammad Hatta proclaim Indonesian independence after Japan's surrender.
  25. 1945-09-02 — Formal Surrender of Japan — The surrender instrument is signed aboard USS Missouri, bringing the Second World War to a formal end.

Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1938-03-12 — Anschluss of Austria — Nazi Germany annexes Austria, accelerating the breakdown of the European security order.
  2. 1938-09-29 — Munich Agreement — Germany, Italy, Britain and France agree to transfer the Sudetenland to Germany without Czechoslovakia taking part in the decisive negotiations.
  3. 1939-03-31 — British and French Guarantees to Poland — After the expansion of German power, the British and French governments declared support for Polish independence. European diplomacy increasingly recognised the danger of another war.
  4. 1939-08-23 — Molotov–Ribbentrop Pact — Nazi Germany and the Soviet Union sign a non-aggression pact with a secret protocol dividing spheres of influence.
  5. 1940-09-27 — Tripartite Pact — Germany, Italy and Japan formalise a military-political alliance.
  6. 1941-03-01 — Bulgaria Joins the Tripartite Pact — In early 1941, diplomatic and military pressure by the Axis powers in the Balkans was increasing. The region was strategically important for future operations against Greece and Yugosla
  7. 1941-07-12 — Anglo-Soviet Agreement on Joint Action — Britain and the Soviet Union agree to cooperate against Nazi Germany.
  8. 1941-08-14 — Atlantic Charter — Roosevelt and Churchill published principles for the post-war international order.
  9. 1941-12-11 — Germany Declares War on the United States — The European and Pacific wars become even more closely connected.
  10. 1942-01-01 — Declaration by United Nations — Twenty-six states reaffirm their common struggle against the Axis powers and pledge not to make a separate peace.
  11. 1943-11-28 — Tehran Conference — The leaders of the Soviet Union, the United States and Britain discuss a common strategy for the war.
  12. 1944-07-01 — Bretton Woods Conference Opens — Delegates work on a new international financial system.
  13. 1944-07-20 — Failed Plot to Assassinate Hitler — A conspiracy by German military and civilian opponents of the regime fails.
  14. 1944-08-21 — Dumbarton Oaks Conference Begins — Representatives of major powers develop plans for the future United Nations.
  15. 1945-02-04 — Yalta Conference — The leaders of the Soviet Union, the United States and Britain discuss the end of the war and post-war Europe.
  16. 1945-04-12 — Death of Franklin D. Roosevelt — The US president dies during the final campaigns of the Second World War.
  17. 1945-04-25 — Meeting on the Elbe — American and Soviet troops meet near Torgau.
  18. 1945-04-25 — San Francisco Conference Opens — Delegates begin discussing the Charter of the United Nations.
  19. 1945-05-07 — German Surrender Signed at Reims — By spring 1945, German forces were losing control across Europe. The leadership of the Third Reich could no longer continue the war on two fronts.
  20. 1945-06-26 — UN Charter Signed — Delegates at the San Francisco Conference sign the Charter of the United Nations.
  21. 1945-07-16 — Trinity Nuclear Test — The United States conducts the world's first detonation of a nuclear device in New Mexico.
  22. 1945-07-17 — Potsdam Conference — The Allied leaders discuss the post-war settlement.
  23. 1945-10-24 — United Nations Formally Established — The Charter of the United Nations enters into force.
  24. 1945-11-20 — Nuremberg Trials Begin — The International Military Tribunal opens proceedings against major Nazi leaders.
  25. 1946-01-10 — First UN General Assembly — The first session of the United Nations General Assembly opens in London.
  26. 1946-01-24 — UN General Assembly Creates an Atomic Energy Commission — The General Assembly adopts its first resolution, addressing atomic energy.
  27. 1946-03-05 — Churchill's Fulton Speech — Winston Churchill delivers his 'Iron Curtain' speech about the division of Europe.
  28. 1946-05-03 — Tokyo War Crimes Tribunal Begins — Proceedings begin against senior Japanese military and political leaders.
  29. 1946-10-01 — Nuremberg Tribunal Judgments — Judgments are delivered against the principal defendants in the international trial.
  30. 1946-10-16 — Executions of Those Sentenced at Nuremberg — Some of the Nazi leaders sentenced to death by the International Military Tribunal are executed.
  31. 1946-12-11 — UNICEF Established — The UN General Assembly creates the children's fund.

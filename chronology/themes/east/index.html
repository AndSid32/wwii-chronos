Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1939-03-15 — Declaration of Independence of Carpatho-Ukraine — The Soim of Carpatho-Ukraine proclaimed independence in Khust.
  2. 1939-09-01 — German Invasion of Poland — The beginning of the Second World War in Europe; Germany attacks from the west.
  3. 1939-09-17 — Soviet Invasion of Poland — The Red Army enters eastern Poland in accordance with the Soviet-German division of spheres of influence.
  4. 1939-09-28 — Surrender of Warsaw — After Germany's attack on Poland, Warsaw came under siege. The city's defenders and civilian population endured heavy artillery fire and bombing.
  5. 1939-11-30 — Winter War — The Soviet Union attacks Finland; fighting continues until March 1940.
  6. 1940-03-12 — Moscow Peace Treaty with Finland — The Winter War ends with a peace treaty under which Finland cedes territory to the Soviet Union.
  7. 1940-06-14 — Soviet Ultimatum to Lithuania — The Soviet Union presented Lithuania with an ultimatum that was followed by Soviet occupation of the country.
  8. 1940-06-28 — Romania Agrees to Cede Bessarabia and Northern Bukovina — In June 1940, the Soviet Union issued Romania an ultimatum demanding Bessarabia and the northern part of Bukovina.
  9. 1941-04-10 — Proclamation of the Independent State of Croatia — After the Axis invasion of Yugoslavia began, the country's political and military system collapsed rapidly.
  10. 1941-06-22 — Operation Barbarossa — Nazi Germany and its allies attack the Soviet Union, opening the largest land front of the war.
  11. 1941-09-08 — Beginning of the Siege of Leningrad — Land routes to Leningrad are cut; the siege will cause mass civilian death.
  12. 1941-09-19 — German Forces Capture Kyiv — The capital of the Ukrainian SSR falls under German occupation.
  13. 1941-10-02 — Operation Typhoon — The German offensive toward Moscow enters its decisive phase.
  14. 1941-12-05 — Soviet Counteroffensive at Moscow — Soviet forces begin a large-scale counteroffensive around Moscow.
  15. 1942-01-07 — Failure of the German Offensive on Moscow — Operation Typhoon fails to achieve its objective as the Soviet counteroffensive pushes Wehrmacht forces back from the capital.
  16. 1942-06-28 — Operation Blue Begins — The Wehrmacht launches its summer offensive in the southern sector of the Eastern Front.
  17. 1942-07-17 — Battle of Stalingrad Begins — Prolonged fighting begins in the Stalingrad area.
  18. 1942-08-23 — Massive Air Raid on Stalingrad — Heavy bombing strikes the city as fighting intensifies in its approaches.
  19. 1942-11-19 — Operation Uranus — Soviet forces launch a counteroffensive at Stalingrad, threatening Axis troops with encirclement.
  20. 1942-11-23 — Encirclement Ring Closes at Stalingrad — Soviet fronts link up near Kalach-on-Don.
  21. 1943-02-02 — Battle of Stalingrad Ends — The remaining forces of the encircled German Sixth Army surrender.
  22. 1943-07-05 — Battle of Kursk — Germany begins an offensive against the Kursk salient; the subsequent Soviet counteroffensive changes the situation on the front.
  23. 1943-09-25 — Red Army Retakes Smolensk — Soviet troops recapture Smolensk after offensive operations.
  24. 1943-11-06 — Liberation of Kyiv — Soviet forces recapture Kyiv from the Nazi occupiers.
  25. 1944-01-27 — Siege of Leningrad Fully Lifted — Soviet forces push German troops away from Leningrad and bring the siege of the city to an end.
  26. 1944-03-19 — Germany Occupies Hungary — By early 1944, Germany's leadership feared that Hungary might try to leave the Axis alliance.
  27. 1944-04-08 — Crimean Offensive — Soviet forces begin driving German and Romanian troops out of Crimea.
  28. 1944-04-10 — Nazi Forces Driven from Odesa — Soviet forces retake the city during the Odesa Offensive.
  29. 1944-06-22 — Operation Bagration — A massive Soviet offensive begins in Belarus.
  30. 1944-08-01 — Warsaw Uprising — The Polish Home Army begins an uprising against German occupation in Warsaw.
  31. 1944-10-02 — Warsaw Uprising Ends — After two months of fighting, Polish insurgents end organised resistance.
  32. 1944-10-06 — Fighting at the Dukla Pass — Soviet and Czechoslovak troops enter Slovakia.
  33. 1945-01-12 — Vistula–Oder Offensive — Soviet forces begin a broad offensive on the road to Berlin.
  34. 1945-04-16 — Berlin Strategic Offensive Operation — Soviet forces begin the final offensive on the capital of Nazi Germany.
  35. 1945-04-30 — Suicide of Adolf Hitler — Adolf Hitler dies in Berlin as the Nazi regime collapses.

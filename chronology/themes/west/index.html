  1. 1939-03-15 — Occupation of the Czech Lands — Germany occupies Bohemia and Moravia after the breakup of Czechoslovakia.
  2. 1939-09-03 — Britain and France Declare War — The two states enter the war against Germany following the invasion of Poland.
  3. 1940-04-09 — Invasion of Denmark and Norway — Germany begins an operation to occupy two Scandinavian states.
  4. 1940-04-14 — Allied Landing at Namsos — British and French forces began landing in central Norway.
  5. 1940-04-27 — Allied Withdrawal from Central Norway — British command begins withdrawing troops from the Trondheim area.
  6. 1940-05-10 — German Offensive in France and the Benelux — German forces attack the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg and France.
  7. 1940-05-14 — Capitulation of the Netherlands — On 10 May 1940, German forces launched an offensive against the Netherlands as part of the wider attack on Western Europe.
  8. 1940-05-26 — Evacuation from Dunkirk — The Allies begin evacuating a large number of troops across the English Channel.
  9. 1940-05-28 — Allies Recapture Narvik — Norwegian and Allied forces regain control of Narvik.
  10. 1940-06-22 — French Armistice with Germany — After France's defeat, an armistice is signed; a large part of the country comes under occupation.
  11. 1942-01-26 — First US Troops Arrive in Northern Ireland — A US Army contingent lands in Northern Ireland after the United States enters the war.
  12. 1942-08-19 — Dieppe Raid — The Allies carry out an unsuccessful amphibious raid on the French coast.
  13. 1942-11-11 — Germany Occupies the Unoccupied Zone of France — After the Allied landings in North Africa, German troops extend the occupation into southern France.
  14. 1944-06-06 — Normandy Landings — D-Day — The Allies open a major front in Western Europe.
  15. 1944-07-25 — Allied Breakout from Normandy — Operation Cobra — After the 6 June 1944 landings, Allied troops fought for weeks in Normandy and struggled to advance rapidly into France.
  16. 1944-08-15 — Allied Landings in Southern France — Allied troops land on France's Mediterranean coast.
  17. 1944-08-25 — Liberation of Paris — French and Allied forces restore control over Paris.
  18. 1944-09-17 — Operation Market Garden — The Allies attempt to seize bridges in the Netherlands with airborne troops and a ground advance.
  19. 1944-12-16 — Battle of the Bulge — Germany launches its last major offensive on the Western Front.
  20. 1945-03-07 — Crossing of the Rhine at Remagen — American forces capture a bridge across the Rhine.
  21. 1945-05-08 — German Surrender Takes Effect — The act of unconditional surrender takes effect on 8 May in Central European time, corresponding to 9 May in Moscow time.

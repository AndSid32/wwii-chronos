Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1943-01-09 — First Flight of the Lockheed Constellation — A future symbol of post-war long-range piston-powered aviation.
  2. 1943-02-18 — Construction Begins on the First Y-12 Production Building at Oak Ridge — Start of a major electromagnetic uranium-isotope separation complex.
  3. 1943-03-05 — First Flight of the Gloster Meteor — The aircraft that would become Britain's first production jet aeroplane.
  4. 1943-03-22 — Major Construction Begins at the Hanford Industrial Complex — Start of the first complex for large-scale plutonium production.

  6. 1943-06-29 — First Flight of the First Production B-29 Built at Wichita — A milestone for one of the most complex mass-produced aircraft of the war.
  7. 1943-08-14 — Big Inch Oil Pipeline Completed — The trunk line stretched for more than 2,000 km.
  8. 1943-09-29 — Polymer Corporation Begins Industrial Production of Synthetic Rubber — Later production capacity exceeded about 36,000 t per year.
  9. 1943-10-19 — Discovery of the Antibiotic Streptomycin — Researchers at Rutgers identified a substance that became important in the treatment of tuberculosis.

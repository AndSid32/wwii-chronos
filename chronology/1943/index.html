Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1943-01-09 — First Flight of the Lockheed Constellation — A future symbol of post-war long-range piston-powered aviation.
  2. 1943-02-02 — Battle of Stalingrad Ends — The remaining forces of the encircled German Sixth Army surrender.
  3. 1943-02-09 — Battle of Guadalcanal Ends — The United States secures control of the island after Japanese forces withdraw.
  4. 1943-02-18 — Construction Begins on the First Y-12 Production Building at Oak Ridge — Start of a major electromagnetic uranium-isotope separation complex.
  5. 1943-03-05 — First Flight of the Gloster Meteor — The aircraft that would become Britain's first production jet aeroplane.
  6. 1943-03-22 — Major Construction Begins at the Hanford Industrial Complex — Start of the first complex for large-scale plutonium production.
  7. 1943-04-09 — Contract Signed to Build ENIAC — The Moore School of Electrical Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania and the US Army sign a contract to develop the ENIAC electronic computer. One of its intended tasks is artil
  8. 1943-04-19 — Warsaw Ghetto Uprising — Jewish underground fighters begin armed resistance to the Nazi liquidation of the ghetto.
  9. 1943-05-13 — Axis Surrender in Tunisia — German and Italian forces end organised resistance in North Africa.
  10. 1943-06-29 — First Flight of the First Production B-29 Built at Wichita — A milestone for one of the most complex mass-produced aircraft of the war.
  11. 1943-07-05 — Battle of Kursk — Germany begins an offensive against the Kursk salient; the subsequent Soviet counteroffensive changes the situation on the front.
  12. 1943-07-10 — Allied Landings in Sicily — The Allies begin a major amphibious invasion of the island.
  13. 1943-07-25 — Fall of Mussolini's Regime — The Italian dictator is removed from office after a decision by the Grand Council of Fascism.
  14. 1943-08-14 — Big Inch Oil Pipeline Completed — The trunk line stretched for more than 2,000 km.
  15. 1943-09-03 — Allied Invasion of Mainland Italy — Anglo-American forces land on the Italian mainland.
  16. 1943-09-08 — Italian Armistice Announced — The Italian government announces an armistice with the Allies; Germany occupies much of the country.
  17. 1943-09-09 — Allied Landings at Salerno — Anglo-American forces land in the Salerno area of mainland Italy.
  18. 1943-09-10 — German Forces Occupy Rome — After the Italian armistice, German troops take control of Rome.
  19. 1943-09-16 — Allied Forces Link Up near Salerno — Allied troops consolidate the Salerno beachhead.
  20. 1943-09-25 — Red Army Retakes Smolensk — Soviet troops recapture Smolensk after offensive operations.
  21. 1943-09-28 — Four Days of Naples Uprising — Residents of Naples rise against German occupation forces.
  22. 1943-09-29 — Polymer Corporation Begins Industrial Production of Synthetic Rubber — Later production capacity exceeded about 36,000 t per year.
  23. 1943-10-01 — Rescue of the Danish Jews — The Danish resistance and ordinary citizens begin a mass effort to ferry Jews to Sweden.
  24. 1943-10-13 — Italy Declares War on Germany — The Italian government enters the war against its former ally on the side of the anti-Hitler coalition.
  25. 1943-10-19 — Discovery of the Antibiotic Streptomycin — Researchers at Rutgers identified a substance that became important in the treatment of tuberculosis.
  26. 1943-11-03 — Willow Run Completes Its 1,000th B-24 Bomber — The 1,000th completed B-24; a precursor to the peak assembly-line rate reached in 1944.
  27. 1943-11-04 — X-10 Graphite Reactor Begins Operation at Oak Ridge — The first reactor to produce plutonium continuously on a pilot-industrial scale.
  28. 1943-11-06 — Liberation of Kyiv — Soviet forces recapture Kyiv from the Nazi occupiers.
  29. 1943-11-28 — Tehran Conference — The leaders of the Soviet Union, the United States and Britain discuss a common strategy for the war.

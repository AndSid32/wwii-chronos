Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1943-09-03 — Allied Invasion of Mainland Italy — Anglo-American forces land on the Italian mainland.
  2. 1943-09-08 — Italian Armistice Announced — The Italian government announces an armistice with the Allies; Germany occupies much of the country.
  3. 1943-09-09 — Allied Landings at Salerno — Anglo-American forces land in the Salerno area of mainland Italy.
  4. 1943-09-10 — German Forces Occupy Rome — After the Italian armistice, German troops take control of Rome.
  5. 1943-09-16 — Allied Forces Link Up near Salerno — Allied troops consolidate the Salerno beachhead.
  6. 1943-09-25 — Red Army Retakes Smolensk — Soviet troops recapture Smolensk after offensive operations.
  7. 1943-09-28 — Four Days of Naples Uprising — Residents of Naples rise against German occupation forces.
  8. 1943-09-29 — Polymer Corporation Begins Industrial Production of Synthetic Rubber — Later production capacity exceeded about 36,000 t per year.

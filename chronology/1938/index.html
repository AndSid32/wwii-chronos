Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1938-01-01 — Creation of SNCF — Unification of France's Main Railways — The state received 51% of the new company's capital.
  2. 1938-03-04 — Dammam No. 7 Strikes Oil in Commercial Quantities — The commercial discovery that launched Saudi Arabia's oil era.
  3. 1938-03-12 — Anschluss of Austria — Nazi Germany annexes Austria, accelerating the breakdown of the European security order.
  4. 1938-03-18 — Mexico Nationalises the Oil Industry — One of the most significant nationalisations of natural resources in the twentieth century.
  5. 1938-04-01 — Nestlé Launches Nescafé — Industrial instant-coffee technology reaches the mass market.
  6. 1938-04-06 — Discovery of Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) — A new polymer with exceptional chemical resistance and very low friction.
  7. 1938-05-23 — Technical Design of the Uglich and Rybinsk Hydroelectric Complexes Approved — Uglich HPP design capacity: 110 MW with two generating units.
  8. 1938-05-26 — Foundation Stone Laid for Volkswagen's Main Plant — A project for one of the largest mass-production automobile factories of its time.
  9. 1938-05-29 — First Anti-Jewish Law in Hungary — The law restricted Jewish participation in economic life.
  10. 1938-07-03 — Mallard Sets the World Steam Locomotive Speed Record — Maximum speed: approximately 203 km/h.
  11. 1938-07-06 — Opening of the Évian Conference on Jewish Refugees — Representatives of states discussed the refugee crisis created by persecution in Nazi-controlled territories.
  12. 1938-07-29 — Battles at Lake Khasan — Fighting broke out between the Red Army and forces of the Japanese Empire along the Soviet-Korean border.
  13. 1938-09-29 — Munich Agreement — Germany, Italy, Britain and France agree to transfer the Sudetenland to Germany without Czechoslovakia taking part in the decisive negotiations.
  14. 1938-10-22 — Chester Carlson Produces the First Xerographic Image — The technological basis for future mass dry-copying of documents.
  15. 1938-10-30 — Orson Welles's 'The War of the Worlds' Radio Broadcast — The radio drama demonstrated both the power and the risks of realistic audio journalism.
  16. 1938-11-09 — Kristallnacht — Mass anti-Jewish pogroms across Germany and annexed Austria on 9–10 November.
  17. 1938-12-02 — First Kindertransport Arrives in Britain — Groups of child refugees from Nazi-controlled territories began arriving in Britain.

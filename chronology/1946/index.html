Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1946-01-10 — First UN General Assembly — The first session of the United Nations General Assembly opens in London.
  2. 1946-01-24 — UN General Assembly Creates an Atomic Energy Commission — The General Assembly adopts its first resolution, addressing atomic energy.
  3. 1946-02-14 — ENIAC Publicly Unveiled — The United States unveils ENIAC, a large-scale electronic digital computer whose calculation speed greatly exceeded that of most contemporary electromechanical systems.
  4. 1946-03-05 — Churchill's Fulton Speech — Winston Churchill delivers his 'Iron Curtain' speech about the division of Europe.
  5. 1946-03-08 — Bell 47 Receives the First Civil Helicopter Certification — It opened the civilian production market for helicopters.
  6. 1946-04-08 — Électricité de France (EDF) Created — More than 1,650 electricity and gas companies were consolidated.
  7. 1946-04-23 — Piaggio Registers the Vespa Industrial Model — 2,484 scooters were produced by the end of 1946.
  8. 1946-05-03 — Tokyo War Crimes Tribunal Begins — Proceedings begin against senior Japanese military and political leaders.
  9. 1946-05-07 — Tokyo Tsushin Kogyo — the Future Sony — Founded — The beginning of one of the emblematic companies of Japan's high-technology industry.
  10. 1946-06-17 — First Commercial Mobile Telephone Service Launched — Practical mobile telephony decades before cellular networks.
  11. 1946-07-04 — Kielce Pogrom — Anti-Jewish violence in post-war Poland kills dozens of people.
  12. 1946-08-01 — Atomic Energy Act of 1946 Signed — The US atomic complex shifts from wartime to civilian federal administration.
  13. 1946-08-02 — First Official Shipment of Reactor-Produced Radioisotopes Sent for Civilian Use — Reactor technology begins moving into medicine, biology and industrial research.
  14. 1946-09-01 — Vickers Viking Enters Regular Service with British European Airways — A new production passenger aircraft for rebuilding Europe's air network.
  15. 1946-09-20 — First Cannes Film Festival Opens — After the wartime interruption, Cannes hosts an international programme of screenings and cultural events.
  16. 1946-10-01 — Nuremberg Tribunal Judgments — Judgments are delivered against the principal defendants in the international trial.
  17. 1946-10-03 — Renault 4CV Publicly Unveiled at the Reopened Paris Motor Show — One of the symbols of post-war mass motorisation in Europe.
  18. 1946-10-16 — Executions of Those Sentenced at Nuremberg — Some of the Nazi leaders sentenced to death by the International Military Tribunal are executed.
  19. 1946-11-04 — UNESCO Constitution Enters into Force — After the required acceptances, UNESCO's founding document acquires legal force.
  20. 1946-12-11 — UNICEF Established — The UN General Assembly creates the children's fund.

Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1942-01-01 — Declaration by United Nations — Twenty-six states reaffirm their common struggle against the Axis powers and pledge not to make a separate peace.
  2. 1942-01-02 — Japanese Forces Capture Manila — Japanese forces enter the Philippine capital; American and Filipino troops withdraw to Bataan.
  3. 1942-01-07 — Failure of the German Offensive on Moscow — Operation Typhoon fails to achieve its objective as the Soviet counteroffensive pushes Wehrmacht forces back from the capital.
  4. 1942-01-14 — First Flight of the Sikorsky XR-4 — Prototype of the First Production Helicopter — In April, the XR-4 reached about 1,520 m; during a delivery flight it covered about 1,225 km.
  5. 1942-01-16 — Deportations from the Łódź Ghetto to Chełmno Begin — The Nazis begin systematic deportations of Jews to be murdered.
  6. 1942-01-20 — Wannsee Conference — Nazi officials coordinate the implementation of the policy to systematically murder Europe's Jews.
  7. 1942-01-23 — Japanese Landing at Rabaul — Japanese forces begin the capture of Rabaul on New Britain.
  8. 1942-01-26 — First US Troops Arrive in Northern Ireland — A US Army contingent lands in Northern Ireland after the United States enters the war.
  9. 1942-01-31 — Allied Forces Leave the Malay Peninsula — British and Allied units withdraw to Singapore after the Japanese advance through Malaya.
  10. 1942-02-15 — Fall of Singapore — The British garrison at Singapore surrenders to Japanese forces.
  11. 1942-03-01 — Second Battle of the Java Sea — Japanese forces destroy Allied ships attempting to escape the Java area.
  12. 1942-03-27 — Deportations of Jews from France to Auschwitz Begin — Deportations from occupied France were directed mainly to Auschwitz.
  13. 1942-04-09 — Surrender on Bataan — American and Filipino forces surrender to Japan; the Bataan Death March follows.
  14. 1942-04-18 — Doolittle Raid — American bombers carry out the first US air strike on the Japanese home islands.
  15. 1942-05-04 — Battle of the Coral Sea — US and Japanese carrier forces fight a battle in which the opposing surface fleets never directly exchange gunfire.
  16. 1942-05-25 — First Commercial Fluid Catalytic Cracking Reactor Starts Up — Feed throughput: about 2.23 million litres per day.
  17. 1942-05-30 — First British Thousand-Bomber Raid on Cologne — In 1942, RAF Bomber Command intensified strategic air attacks on Germany's industrial cities.
  18. 1942-06-04 — Battle of Midway — The US Navy wins a major victory over Japanese carrier forces.
  19. 1942-06-28 — Operation Blue Begins — The Wehrmacht launches its summer offensive in the southern sector of the Eastern Front.
  20. 1942-07-15 — Systematic Deportations of Jews from the Netherlands Begin — Transports departed for the killing centres.
  21. 1942-07-17 — Battle of Stalingrad Begins — Prolonged fighting begins in the Stalingrad area.
  22. 1942-07-18 — Messerschmitt Me 262 Makes Its First Flight on Jet Power Alone — A key milestone for the first production jet fighter.
  23. 1942-07-22 — Grossaktion in the Warsaw Ghetto Begins — Mass deportations to Treblinka begin.
  24. 1942-08-07 — Landings on Guadalcanal — The United States begins an offensive operation in the Solomon Islands.
  25. 1942-08-13 — Manhattan Engineer District Formally Established — Organisational start of the Oak Ridge and Hanford industrial sites.
  26. 1942-08-19 — Dieppe Raid — The Allies carry out an unsuccessful amphibious raid on the French coast.
  27. 1942-08-23 — Massive Air Raid on Stalingrad — Heavy bombing strikes the city as fighting intensifies in its approaches.
  28. 1942-10-03 — First Successful Full-Scale Test of the V-2 Rocket — Length about 14 m; mass about 13.2 t; speed over 5,600 km/h.
  29. 1942-10-23 — Second Battle of El Alamein — Allied forces go on the offensive in a battle that becomes a turning point in North Africa.
  30. 1942-11-08 — Operation Torch — Anglo-American forces land in French North Africa.
  31. 1942-11-11 — Germany Occupies the Unoccupied Zone of France — After the Allied landings in North Africa, German troops extend the occupation into southern France.
  32. 1942-11-12 — Liberty Ship SS Robert E. Peary Sets a Construction-Speed Record — From keel laying to launch: 4 days, 15 hours, 29 minutes.
  33. 1942-11-19 — Operation Uranus — Soviet forces launch a counteroffensive at Stalingrad, threatening Axis troops with encirclement.
  34. 1942-11-20 — Alaska Highway Officially Opened — Original route: more than 2,400 km.
  35. 1942-11-23 — Encirclement Ring Closes at Stalingrad — Soviet fronts link up near Kalach-on-Don.
  36. 1942-12-02 — First Controlled Self-Sustaining Nuclear Chain Reaction — Under Enrico Fermi's leadership, Chicago Pile-1 achieved the first controlled self-sustaining nuclear chain reaction, a major milestone in experimental nuclear physics.

World War II Timeline: January 1942

A dated guide to 9 documented events in January 1942, with links to individual historical records.

Chronology index · Interactive timeline · WWII CHRONOS

Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1942-01-01 — Declaration by United Nations — Twenty-six states reaffirm their common struggle against the Axis powers and pledge not to make a separate peace.
  2. 1942-01-02 — Japanese Forces Capture Manila — Japanese forces enter the Philippine capital; American and Filipino troops withdraw to Bataan.
  3. 1942-01-07 — Failure of the German Offensive on Moscow — Operation Typhoon fails to achieve its objective as the Soviet counteroffensive pushes Wehrmacht forces back from the capital.
  4. 1942-01-14 — First Flight of the Sikorsky XR-4 — Prototype of the First Production Helicopter — In April, the XR-4 reached about 1,520 m; during a delivery flight it covered about 1,225 km.
  5. 1942-01-16 — Deportations from the Łódź Ghetto to Chełmno Begin — The Nazis begin systematic deportations of Jews to be murdered.
  6. 1942-01-20 — Wannsee Conference — Nazi officials coordinate the implementation of the policy to systematically murder Europe's Jews.
  7. 1942-01-23 — Japanese Landing at Rabaul — Japanese forces begin the capture of Rabaul on New Britain.
  8. 1942-01-26 — First US Troops Arrive in Northern Ireland — A US Army contingent lands in Northern Ireland after the United States enters the war.
  9. 1942-01-31 — Allied Forces Leave the Malay Peninsula — British and Allied units withdraw to Singapore after the Japanese advance through Malaya.

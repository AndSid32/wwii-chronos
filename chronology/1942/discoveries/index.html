Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1942-01-14 — First Flight of the Sikorsky XR-4 — Prototype of the First Production Helicopter — In April, the XR-4 reached about 1,520 m; during a delivery flight it covered about 1,225 km.
  2. 1942-05-25 — First Commercial Fluid Catalytic Cracking Reactor Starts Up — Feed throughput: about 2.23 million litres per day.
  3. 1942-07-18 — Messerschmitt Me 262 Makes Its First Flight on Jet Power Alone — A key milestone for the first production jet fighter.
  4. 1942-08-13 — Manhattan Engineer District Formally Established — Organisational start of the Oak Ridge and Hanford industrial sites.
  5. 1942-10-03 — First Successful Full-Scale Test of the V-2 Rocket — Length about 14 m; mass about 13.2 t; speed over 5,600 km/h.
  6. 1942-11-12 — Liberty Ship SS Robert E. Peary Sets a Construction-Speed Record — From keel laying to launch: 4 days, 15 hours, 29 minutes.
  7. 1942-11-20 — Alaska Highway Officially Opened — Original route: more than 2,400 km.
  8. 1942-12-02 — First Controlled Self-Sustaining Nuclear Chain Reaction — Under Enrico Fermi's leadership, Chicago Pile-1 achieved the first controlled self-sustaining nuclear chain reaction, a major milestone in experimental nuclear physics.

Dated historical records

Entries below follow the dates recorded in the WWII CHRONOS archive. Each linked title opens the original historical entry, including its source reference.

  1. 1940-02-21 — High-Power Cavity Magnetron Successfully Tested in Birmingham — The technological foundation for compact microwave radar.
  2. 1940-02-23 — Russell Ohl Observes the p–n Junction in Silicon — A fundamental milestone for the future semiconductor industry.
  3. 1940-03-12 — Moscow Peace Treaty with Finland — The Winter War ends with a peace treaty under which Finland cedes territory to the Soviet Union.
  4. 1940-04-09 — Invasion of Denmark and Norway — Germany begins an operation to occupy two Scandinavian states.
  5. 1940-04-10 — First Naval Battle of Narvik — British destroyers attacked German ships in Narvik harbour.
  6. 1940-04-13 — Second Naval Battle of Narvik — A British force again attacked German destroyers in the Norwegian fjords.
  7. 1940-04-14 — Allied Landing at Namsos — British and French forces began landing in central Norway.
  8. 1940-04-27 — Allied Withdrawal from Central Norway — British command begins withdrawing troops from the Trondheim area.
  9. 1940-04-30 — Sealing of the Łódź Ghetto — The German administration isolated the city's Jewish population.
  10. 1940-05-10 — German Offensive in France and the Benelux — German forces attack the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg and France.
  11. 1940-05-14 — Capitulation of the Netherlands — On 10 May 1940, German forces launched an offensive against the Netherlands as part of the wider attack on Western Europe.
  12. 1940-05-15 — Nylon Stockings Go on Mass Sale — Synthetic fibres enter the mass consumer market.
  13. 1940-05-26 — Evacuation from Dunkirk — The Allies begin evacuating a large number of troops across the English Channel.
  14. 1940-05-28 — Allies Recapture Narvik — Norwegian and Allied forces regain control of Narvik.
  15. 1940-06-10 — Italy Enters the War — Mussolini's regime declares war on France and Britain.
  16. 1940-06-14 — Soviet Ultimatum to Lithuania — The Soviet Union presented Lithuania with an ultimatum that was followed by Soviet occupation of the country.
  17. 1940-06-22 — French Armistice with Germany — After France's defeat, an armistice is signed; a large part of the country comes under occupation.
  18. 1940-06-28 — Romania Agrees to Cede Bessarabia and Northern Bukovina — In June 1940, the Soviet Union issued Romania an ultimatum demanding Bessarabia and the northern part of Bukovina.
  19. 1940-07-10 — Battle of Britain — The Luftwaffe begins a major air campaign against Britain.
  20. 1940-08-27 — First Flight of the Caproni Campini N.1 Experimental Jet Aircraft — One of the earliest full-scale demonstrations of jet propulsion.
  21. 1940-08-29 — Marl Plant Produces Its First Bales of Buna Synthetic Rubber — Initial annual capacity: approximately 18,000 t.
  22. 1940-09-07 — Beginning of the London Blitz — German aircraft begin a series of mass raids on London.
  23. 1940-09-11 — Bell Labs Publicly Demonstrates Remote Computing over Telephone Lines — An early demonstration of remote access to a computing machine.
  24. 1940-09-23 — American Bantam Delivers the First Prototype of the Future Jeep — 49 days elapsed from order to prototype delivery.
  25. 1940-09-27 — Tripartite Pact — Germany, Italy and Japan formalise a military-political alliance.
  26. 1940-10-28 — Italian Invasion of Greece — Italian forces invade Greece from occupied Albania.
  27. 1940-11-15 — Warsaw Ghetto Sealed — The German occupation authorities isolate Jews inside the Warsaw ghetto.
